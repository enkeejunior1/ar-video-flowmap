Technical challenges

FlowMap distillation (\(\pi\)-Flow, MeanFlow, consistency distillation) is widely used for few-step image and bidirectional video models. However, why does FlowMap fail on AR video? We discovered two failure modes of FlowMap distillation on AR video and set out to solve them.

Method

We diagnose the causes of drifting and low-quality motion, and fix each issue with a new guidance term. This guidance plugs directly into the FlowMap objective (\(\pi\)-Flow, MeanFlow or consistency distillation).

Anti-drifting guidance

When the student generates an erroneous history, the AR teacher has no way to correct it. We train a drift model that imitates the student's errors and use it as the negative term of guidance, giving the student a supervision signal that recovers from its errors.

Motion-aware guidance

We find that the teacher's velocity field is non-smooth on patches corresponding to motion. Observing that skip-layer guidance smooths this field, we design motion-aware guidance to focus it more strongly on moving patches.

cf. Lipschitz constant of the teacher's velocity field

The Lipschitz constant bounds how fast the teacher's velocity \(v\) changes as \(x_t\) moves. The less smooth this velocity field is, the more complex a function the student has to learn, making training harder.

Experiments

Main results

5 s video generation

On 5 s videos, our \(\pi\)-Flow students match the DMD students, which are distilled from 14B teachers, on VBench (84.87 and 84.76) without any DMD stage. They also have the highest Quality scores and are more diverse across seeds.

Long video generation

On 30 s videos with the Causal-rCM teacher, all three of our students lose less imaging quality over 30 s than the DMD student, nearly halving its drop (ΔIQ). The 30 s videos are at the top of the page.

Ablation study

★Anti-drifting guidance does not hurt diversity

Unlike the reverse KL of DMD on \(x_0\), anti-drifting guidance does not reduce diversity in practice: with it, the student is more diverse across seeds and also scores higher on VBench.

Motion-aware guidance

Motion-aware guidance scores higher than skip-layer guidance with any fixed weight, including the weight that matches its mean.

Anti-drifting guidance

Without anti-drifting guidance, both students drift more over 30 s (ΔIQ and ΔAQ) and score lower on 5 s VBench.

Motion-aware guidance + anti-drifting guidance

Each term alone raises VBench Total by more than 2.5 points over CFG only; with both, the student matches the DMD student.
